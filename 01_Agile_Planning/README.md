## Reading the workbook

The workbook has nine sheets. `Project Backlog` and the four `Sprint n Backlog`
sheets are the planning record. `README` and `Assessment Mapping` explain the
conventions; `Risks & Assumptions` holds the risk register.

One sheet, `答辩速查`, is a viva preparation script rather than planning
evidence. It is not referenced by any assessed document, and given the
assessment's position on generative AI it is safer to delete it from the
submitted workbook than to leave it in the planning evidence.
